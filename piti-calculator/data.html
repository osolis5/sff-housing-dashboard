Data repository & methods

The source workbook

Dataset values come from one file: “Chicago Community Areas_2026_SFF_Indicators_07022026.xlsx” (the July 2026 data repository), compiled for the Steans Family Foundation with underlying data from the Institute for Housing Studies at DePaul University. One value pair — median homebuyer incomes — comes from the accompanying IHS baseline data presentation, slide 21 (HMDA), and is cited below where used. The workbook covers nine West/Southwest Side community areas plus the City of Chicago; the calculator uses the North Lawndale rows throughout. Cell references below are 1-indexed (column letter + row number) within each named sheet.

  • A.Demographic_Socioeconomic — households and incomes; American Community Survey 5-year estimates (2010 & 2024 vintages).
  • B.Taxpayer_Characteristics — median property-tax bills and exemptions; Cook County Treasurer / Assessor records, Tax Years 2012–2024.
  • E.Property_Sales — recorded sales counts and median prices by property type, 2005–1H 2025.
  • G.Mortgage_Lending — Home Mortgage Disclosure Act (HMDA) first-lien, owner-occupied, 1–4-unit home-purchase originations, 2019 / 2023 / 2024 — including the 2024 mortgage-financed price tiers.

Adjustable assumptions — not dataset values

These defaults are starting points for exploration, clearly badged assumption in the calculator and editable by the user. They are not drawn from the workbook.

AssumptionDefaultBasis
Effective property-tax rate1.5% of price / yrSeeds the tax field: annual taxes = 1.5% × purchase price, recomputed live until the field is edited. Based on the Civic Federation's effective-tax-rate analysis for the City of Chicago. A buyer's actual bill depends on assessment, levies, and exemptions.
Interest rate6.5%Placeholder in the range of recent 30-year fixed averages; check Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey for current rates.
Down payment5%Common conventional first-time-buyer range (FHA minimum is 3.5%). Adjustable 0–50%.
Homeowners insurance$1,700 / yrRound-number placeholder; premiums vary widely by property and carrier.
PMI0.75% of loan / yrMid-range private-mortgage-insurance cost, applied only below 20% down; actual quotes vary by credit and LTV.
Housing share of income28%Long-standing underwriting front-end guideline; adjustable 20–45%.
Cost-burden thresholds30% / 50%HUD definitions: paying over 30% of income for housing = cost-burdened; over 50% = severely burdened.

Methods

Monthly payment (PITI)

loan L = price × (1 − down%) monthly rate r = APR / 12 n = years × 12 P&I = L · r(1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1) (standard amortization) taxes = 1.5% × price per year (default; live until edited, then fixed $) PITI = P&I + taxes/12 + insurance/12 + PMI/12 PMI = 0.75% × L per year, only while down% < 20

Affordability

income needed = PITI × 12 ÷ (housing share %) burden (per profile) = PITI ÷ (household income / 12) max price — taxes in default 1.5%-rate mode (taxes scale with the candidate price): = (income/12 × share% − ins/12) ÷ [(1 − down%) × (amortization factor + PMI factor) + 1.5%/12] max price — taxes edited to a fixed $ amount: = (income/12 × share% − taxes/12 − ins/12) ÷ [(1 − down%) × (amortization factor + PMI factor)] gap (per profile) = max price − this scenario's purchase price (positive = headroom above this price; negative = shortfall)
  • Rounding. Dollar figures display rounded to whole dollars; derived values (income needed, burden) are computed from unrounded intermediates, so they can differ by a few dollars from arithmetic on the displayed numbers.
  • Bracket marker. The red marker on the income-bracket bar sits at the boundary of the bracket containing the required income (brackets are ranges, so position within a bracket is not interpolated). The “households at or above” share sums whole brackets only — no within-bracket estimation.
  • Vintages. Price tiers and homebuyer incomes are 2024 HMDA; all-sales medians are 2024; tax-bill context is TY2024 (latest available); renter/household incomes are 2024 ACS 5-year estimates; HMDA borrower mixes pool 2023–24. These are the most recent values of each series.
